Valtory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Valtory is a personal money tracker. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone: transactions, accounts, categories, monthly counts and settings. The developer has no server and never receives your data.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only for:

  1. Exchange rates. It downloads public currency rates from open.er-api.com, with cdn.jsdelivr.net as a fallback. No personal data is sent; these are plain requests for the latest rates.
  2. Your own Google account (optional). If you tap "Connect Google account", the app uses Google's permission drive.file. With it, the app:
    • saves a daily backup file in a "Valtory" folder in your Google Drive;
    • optionally keeps a view-only Google Sheet of your data in that same folder (the app writes it and never reads it back).

    With this permission, the app can only access files it created. It can't see any of your other Drive files. The data goes directly from your phone to your Google account. The developer can't see it. Google handles it under Google's Privacy Policy.

Android backup

If Android's own backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. This is managed by Android and Google, not by the developer.

No tracking

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • In Google Drive: delete the "Valtory" folder. To revoke the app's access, go to Google Account > Security > Third-party apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
